# Impact missions and estimates

Each career has three sequential milestones, with three alternative actions at each milestone. The table lists the first task option. The second and third options are different work subjects, retaining their existing reward profiles (1.4× / about 0.75×, and 0.8× / about 1.35×) for save and balance continuity. These multipliers do not label learning styles. Each option has explicit rewards in the TOML.

Delivery option rewards:

| Stage | Mastery gate | One-time mastery reward | Added practice rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Practitioner | 300 | 80 | 0.3/sec |
| Team leader | 2,000 | 200 | 0.7/sec |
| Career legacy | 7,000 | 400 | 1.2/sec |

Choose one of three distinct contributions to complete a milestone. Only the task matching a numeric scenario carries that model; the other tasks record their own qualitative outcomes. Read `11-distinct-career-tasks.md` for the current action design.

Role training and all job work samples must be complete. Each later mission requires the preceding mission. Repeated clicks cannot duplicate rewards. Missions remain available after the existing 10,000-mastery completion condition. Completing missions is a simulated accomplishment, not evidence of actual clinical or professional competence.

The TOML contains 11 models with named parameters, arithmetic expressions, assumptions, limitations, and methodology links. The compiler safely evaluates arithmetic into browser metrics. Inputs are illustrative scenario assumptions, not numbers asserted to have been measured by the linked organizations.

- **Bridge:** 60,000 vehicles/day × 365 × 100 years = **2.19 billion crossings**. At 1.3 occupants and 10 minutes saved, **474.5 million person-hours**. At an assumed $20/hour, **$9.49 billion gross undiscounted time benefit**, before costs and discounting. Not trillions of incremental GDP.
- **Congenital-heart story:** assumed 60 remaining years after successful treatment versus 2 untreated = **58 conditional additional life-years**. No named condition or guaranteed prognosis; expected clinical benefit requires survival and risk evidence.
- **Solar:** 1 GW, 25% capacity factor, 25 years, 0.5% annual degradation gives about **51.59 million MWh**. Assumed net displacement of 0.4 t/MWh gives about **20.64 million tonnes CO₂ avoided**. Actual dispatch and future grid emissions can change this.
- **Prevention:** 100,000 people × 20% baseline risk × 20% relative reduction = **4,000 expected events prevented**, not 20,000.
- **Vaccination:** one million people × 80% coverage × 5% disease risk × 90% efficacy = **36,000 expected cases prevented**; cases are not deaths.

Other models cover water delivery, building efficiency, chip energy savings, restoration, logistics spoilage, and service time. Infrastructure and care outcomes are team achievements. The same bridge or program model can illustrate several contributing careers; **never sum those representations as separate projects**. No cross-career impact total is implemented.
