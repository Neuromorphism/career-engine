# Results and coverage

The original prototype contained **6 fields, 49 nodes, and 29 job leaves**. It now contains **15 fields, 261 nodes, and 182 job leaves**. Every original node ID and selected path remains valid.

The original six fields remain first: Engineering, Medicine, Law, Skilled Trades, Science, Business. Added fields: Education & Libraries; Arts, Design & Media; Community & Public Service; Agriculture, Forestry & Land Stewardship; Hospitality & Food; Personal Care & Everyday Services; Sales & Administration; Transportation & Logistics Operations; Military Service & Civilian Transition.

Deepened examples include digital chip implementation, control systems, water treatment, geotechnical and bridge engineering, robotics, aerospace, biomedical devices, internal-medicine specialties, advanced cardiac intervention, congenital-heart surgery, specialist surgery, diagnostics, rehabilitation, multiple legal practices, advanced trades, chemistry, ecology, quantitative science, and finance.

Coverage includes healthcare support, cleaning and grounds work, agricultural field work, caregiving, administration, and customer service. These are independent valuable careers, not merely inferior stages on a degree ladder. All 23 SOC major groups have at least one mapped playable endpoint; this does **not** mean every occupation in each group is included.

Each occupation contains references, mapping precision, entry routes, later-life options, a four-or-more-step case curriculum, a bounded work sample, and three impact missions. Some specialized game titles map to a broader O*NET occupation or related family. That uncertainty is explicit.

Seven researched individual paths are recorded under `[[life_path_examples]]`: Mae Jemison, Mary Barra, Ursula Burns, José Andrés, Temple Grandin, John B. Goodenough, and Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie. They illustrate cross-disciplinary preparation, co-op/internship entry, leadership, enterprise, service, education change, and parallel practice. Several biographical specialties extend beyond current playable coverage; related game IDs are thematic connections, not assertions of exact employment titles or credentials.
