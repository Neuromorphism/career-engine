# Authoritative sources

All URLs and access dates are recorded in the TOML `[[sources]]` registry. Research date: 2026-10-14.

| Source | Role in the investigation |
|---|---|
| [BLS SOC 2018](https://www.bls.gov/soc/2018/major_groups.htm) | US statistical hierarchy; 23 major groups and 867 detailed occupations. |
| [O*NET-SOC 2019 listings](https://www.onetcenter.org/taxonomy/2019/list.html) | Government-supported detailed occupation reference: 1,016 taxonomy titles, including 923 data-level occupations. This is the recovered government-list starting point. |
| [ESCO occupation pillar](https://esco.ec.europa.eu/en/classification/occupation_main) | Occupation/skill relationships and ISCO-08 structure. The portal identified version 1.2.1 during research. Referenced, not fully imported. |
| [ILO ISCO-08](https://isco.ilo.org/en/isco-08/) | International four-level structure with 436 unit groups. Referenced, not used as an invented crosswalk. |
| [BLS Occupation Finder](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/occupation-finder.htm) | Typical education, experience, and training; occupation-specific profiles support route distinctions. |
| [DOL apprenticeship guidance](https://www.apprenticeship.gov/help/what-apprenticeship) | Paid work, instruction, mentorship, and credentials; program-specific recognition of prior experience. |
| [NCEES licensure](https://ncees.org/licensure) | Engineering education, experience, and examinations; jurisdiction-specific authority. |
| [ABTS thoracic pathways](https://www.abts.org/ABTS/CertificationWebPages/Pathways%20to%20Certification.aspx) | Multiple approved thoracic surgery training routes. |
| [ABTS congenital pathway](https://www.abts.org/ABTS/Congenital/Congenital_Pathway_1/Certification_Pathway.aspx) | Congenital fellowship and relevant certification gates. |
| [NCSBN nursing requirements](https://ncsbn.org/nursing-regulation/education/board-of-nursing-licensure-requirements.page) | Distinct RN, PN, and advanced-practice requirements by jurisdiction. |

BLS profiles for physicians, lawyers, teachers, social workers, librarians, pilots, farmers, chefs, and software developers are in the registry. Older Career Outlook articles support process and route patterns; their historical pay or projections are not used as current data.

The [O*NET 31.0 database license](https://www.onetcenter.org/license_db.html) specifies CC BY 4.0 attribution and disclosure of modifications. The source records USDOL/ETA provenance and distinguishes authored additions. O*NET references and codes are curated mappings, not a wholesale database import. Existing employer posting links inherited from the prototype were not comprehensively re-audited for continued availability.
