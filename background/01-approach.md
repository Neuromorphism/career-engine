# Investigation approach

Keep five concepts separate:

1. **Occupation:** a bundle of real work, referenced to an authoritative classification.
2. **Specialty:** a narrower domain that may share one statistical occupation with many other jobs.
3. **Entry route:** education, apprenticeship, prior experience, supervised practice, and any authorization needed to enter the work.
4. **Professional-life move:** specialization, technical expertise, management, ownership, teaching, lateral change, return after a break, migration, or simultaneous work.
5. **Game mission:** an authored scenario with prerequisites and a consequential result.

Use US SOC for broad coverage, O*NET-SOC for detailed occupation references, ESCO for occupation/skill relationships, and ISCO for international structural context. These classifications describe work; they do not prescribe a universal promotion ladder. No unverified ESCO identifier or SOC-to-ISCO crosswalk is assigned.

For routes, prefer government occupational profiles, licensing bodies, apprenticeship guidance, and primary biographical sources. Record mandatory legal gates separately from typical employer expectations. Distinguish an educational change from a move between qualified professions.

The 54 transition edges are plausible authored connections with named transferable skills and remaining requirements. They are not claimed to be empirically measured transition frequencies. Seven documented biographies test whether the design accommodates non-linear lives; famous examples are not a representative sample.

Preserve different forms of accomplishment. Advanced technical practice, a reliable neighborhood service, humane care, teaching, and institutional leadership should all have meaningful late-game outcomes. Management is optional, and income is not the sole impact measure.

For numeric outcomes, define the baseline, population, time horizon, units, adoption assumptions, costs or omissions, and attribution. Compute the arithmetic reproducibly. Do not turn throughput into new GDP or conditional clinical stories into guaranteed prognosis.
