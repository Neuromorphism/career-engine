# Remaining investigations

Prioritize these without treating the current source as exhaustive:

- Import and reconcile the full official O*NET-SOC listing and SOC hierarchy through a reproducible, licensed ingestion step. Current mappings are curated; the entire original dataset is not vendored.
- Audit every specialist-to-occupation mapping against scope and tasks. Replace broad family references where an exact occupation exists; do not fabricate one-to-one matches.
- Add missing specialties: advanced nursing, physician assistants, occupational and speech therapy, laboratory and imaging technicians, psychology, social sciences, architecture and planning, literary writing, interpreting, surveying, utilities, mining, maritime engineering, food science, and more production crafts.
- Research role-specific credential routes instead of relying on family-wide templates. Teaching, nursing, public engineering, law, medicine, pilots, and clinical social work already have separate gate patterns; remaining roles need comparable detail.
- Add varied documented paths from ordinary practitioners, including rural work, adult apprenticeships, technical-college routes, caregiving breaks, disability accommodations, immigration, job loss, cooperative ownership, and career returns. Current biographies overrepresent prominent high achievers.
- Add actual ESCO skill identifiers and verified crosswalks with version and license provenance. Current ESCO/ISCO references establish approach, not a completed international mapping.
- Model country-specific routes. Current credential summaries are primarily US-oriented and cannot be treated as universal rules.
- Develop playable lateral transitions and portfolio careers if requested. Existing transition records are investigation content; a new run currently explores a new branch.
- Ground major project assumptions in actual comparable projects and published evaluation data. Current calculations are transparent scenarios. Add local demand, costs, discount rates, survival curves, adoption, uncertainty intervals, and independent outcome evidence.
- Improve new-role curricula with more fully authored cases and give new AI-rival branches carefully justified profiles. Many new curricula share a review structure even though their missions and occupational contexts differ.

No salaries, transition likelihoods, job guarantees, universal credential durations, or independently measured causal impact were invented. Preserve that distinction when expanding.
